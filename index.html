FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my own domain?

No. You get a free address to share — like yoursalon.vercel.app. If you already have a domain, we can point it at your Kaya page, but it's optional.

Do my clients need to install anything?

No. They open the link, pick a service, pick a time, enter their name and number. No app, no account, no password.

What happens to my client data?

It's yours. You can export it at any time. If you leave Kaya, we don't hold it hostage — you get a full CSV of every client and every booking.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Every salon that joins the waitlist gets a 30-day free trial once we open the doors. No card needed to start.

When is it launching?

We're onboarding the first 20 salons in the coming months. The waitlist is first-come, first-served. Join and we'll email you when it's your turn.
